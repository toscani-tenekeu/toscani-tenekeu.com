Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit toscani-tenekeu.com. In short: the site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

What is collected

Like almost every website, the server that delivers these pages writes a technical log for each request. A log line contains:

  • your IP address;
  • the date and time of the request;
  • the page or file requested;
  • your browser's user agent (browser and system name);
  • the referring page, when your browser sends it.

These logs are used only to keep the site running and secure, for example to investigate errors or abuse. They are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts. Fonts and images are served from this server, so loading the page does not contact any other company.

Hosting

The site is hosted by KmerHosting, which runs the server where the logs above are stored.

Email

If you write to hello@toscani-tenekeu.com, your message and address are used only to reply to you. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

Links to other websites

This site links to social networks and other websites (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitch, X, Discord, Facebook, LinkedIn, GitHub and others). Once you follow a link, the other site's own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You can ask what information about you is held, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to hello@toscani-tenekeu.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
